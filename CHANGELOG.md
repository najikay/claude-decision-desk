# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-14
- First release: decide-frame, decide-compare, decide-gonogo, decide-premortem, decide-review.
- One rule in every skill, word for word: no fact about your options is invented, the weights are yours to change, and what would flip the answer is always named.
- Every reply leaves something to keep (a decision frame, a weighted matrix with its sensitivity line, a go/no-go card, a pre-mortem table with a kill criterion, a rescored record or a look-back card), one shared decision record that any skill can read back, and one **Next:** line with the words to type.
- Twelve invented eval cases (Dana's two offers for compare and rescore; a job-or-master's tangle; flats in Hebrew; a café job in Arabic; a used car, and its go/no-go record pasted back after the inspection; a move; a startup look-back; a medication question; a bare laptop question; a "just tell me"), Sonnet judge.
