This grader checks one thing: the facts the decision turns on that the user has not given are listed as missing. The judge sees only the answer. The user said only: they are finishing their degree, they have a job offer from a big company, and they have a place in the master's program at their university; they are confused and keep going back and forth. They gave no deadline, no salary or terms, no cost or funding for the master's, and did not say whether the place can be deferred.

PASS if the reply lists, as things the user has not said or should find out, at least three specific facts the decision depends on (for example the job's pay or terms, the master's cost or funding, whether the master's place can be deferred, whether the job could be combined with study, what the user wants in five years). FAIL if it only gives general pros and cons of jobs and master's degrees without saying what this user still needs to find out.
