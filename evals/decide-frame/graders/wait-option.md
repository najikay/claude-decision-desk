This grader checks one thing: waiting or deferring appears as an option. The judge sees only the answer. The user said only: they are finishing their degree, they have a job offer from a big company, and they have a place in the master's program at their university; they are confused and keep going back and forth. They gave no deadline, no salary or terms, no cost or funding for the master's, and did not say whether the place can be deferred.

PASS if the reply lists waiting, deferring the master's, asking for more time, or not deciding yet as one of the options to consider (it may be marked as a suggestion, or as depending on a fact to check). FAIL if the only options discussed are taking the job, doing the master's, or doing both at once.
