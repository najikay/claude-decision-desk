This grader checks one thing: the causes of failure are drawn from the user's own facts. The judge sees only the answer. The user said: they have decided to take the job and move to a new city, starting in six weeks. The four facts they gave: (1) they don't know anyone there; (2) rent will take 45% of their take-home pay; (3) the contract is 12 months with a 3-month trial period; (4) their partner is staying behind and they plan to visit every second weekend, a 3-hour train ride each way. Nothing else was said about the city, the company, the job, the pay or the partner.

PASS if at least three of those four facts appear as, or inside, the reasons the plan could fail (for example the 45% rent leaving little margin, the 3-month trial, the every-second-weekend 3-hour trips, knowing no one). FAIL if fewer than three of the four facts are used in the causes, or if the causes are a generic list that would fit any move.
