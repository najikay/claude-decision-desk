Two years ago I chose a job at a 40-person startup over an offer from a bank. I compared both on pay, learning and commute, talked to two people who worked at each place, and asked the startup about its funding: they said they had 18 months of runway, which was true. Last month the startup shut down after its main customer cancelled its contract, which was only announced last month. Now I'm unemployed and my friend who took a job at the bank just got promoted. Did I make the wrong call? I regret it so much.
