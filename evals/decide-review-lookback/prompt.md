Two years ago I picked a job at a 40-person startup over an offer from a bank. I compared both on pay, learning and commute, talked to two people who worked at each place, and asked the startup about its funding. They told me they had 18 months of runway. Last month the startup shut down because its main customer cancelled their contract. I should have seen that coming. Now I'm unemployed and my friend who took the bank job just got promoted. I made the wrong call, didn't I? I regret it so much.
