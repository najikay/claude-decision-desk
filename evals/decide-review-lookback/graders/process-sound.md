This grader checks one thing: the decision process is called sound. The judge sees only the answer. The user said: two years ago they chose a job at a 40-person startup over an offer from a bank. They compared both on pay, learning and commute, talked to two people who worked at each, and asked the startup about its funding; the startup said it had 18 months of runway, which was true. Last month the startup shut down after its main customer cancelled its contract, which was announced only last month. The user is now unemployed, a friend who took a job at the bank was promoted, and the user asks whether they made the wrong call and says they regret it.

PASS if the reply says plainly that the way the user decided was sound, reasonable or careful (naming at least one of the steps they took: comparing on criteria, talking to people at each place, checking the funding). FAIL if it does not judge the process, or calls the process poor.
