This grader checks one thing: nothing is invented about the job, the money or the exams. The judge sees only the answer, which is in Arabic. The user wrote in Arabic, asking whether to quit their part-time job at a café. The only facts they gave: they work 12 hours a week and earn 1,800 shekels a month, which covers transport and food; final exams start in five weeks; their average is 78 and they want to raise it to 85 to apply for a master's; the café owner is kind to them.

PASS if every fact the reply states about the job, the pay, the exams or the owner comes from those facts, or is arithmetic on them (12 hours a week for five weeks is 60 hours; 1,800 a month), with the numbers 12, 1,800, 78 and 85 kept as given (in the same digits the user used), or is plainly marked as unknown, a question to ask, an assumption, or general knowledge. FAIL if it states as fact anything not given, such as other jobs being easy or hard to find, typical wages, how many study hours it takes to raise an average, that the owner would or would not agree to a change, the user's savings, or a changed figure for any of the four numbers.
