# Privacy

Decision Desk is a set of instructions (skills) for Claude. This page says what that means for your data.

## What Decision Desk collects

Nothing. The plugin contains no code that runs for you, no server and no tracking. Its author receives no data from your use of it: not your options, not your numbers, not your decision records, not your conversations, not the fact that you used it.

## What it reads

When you use a skill, Claude reads what you put into the conversation: the decision you are facing, the options, the facts you give about them, what matters to you, and any decision record you paste back. Decision Desk only tells Claude how to work with them. A decision usually holds personal data: pay, rent and savings, health, family and relationships, an employer's or a landlord's name. Give only what the decision needs; an employer can be "company A" and a sum can be rounded if you prefer.

## Where your data goes

- Your situation stays in the chat. Your conversation, including anything you type or paste, is handled by Anthropic under the terms and privacy policy of the Claude product you are using. Decision Desk does not change that and adds no other recipient.
- The skills instruct Claude never to put your situation into a web search.
- No skill writes a file unless you ask for one. The matrix, the cards and the decision record are text you copy yourself.
- Nothing is kept between conversations by the plugin. The decision record is what you keep; paste it back to pick up where you left off.

## What you can do

- Give only the facts the decision needs, and use placeholders for names if you like.
- Delete a conversation in Claude to remove it, under the terms of your Claude product.
